Microduck · Low-cost bipedal robot replica

Since Oct. 2026

I am building a low-cost replica of Microduck, an open-source bipedal robot duck. The hardware follows the XGO-Duck open-source design, using 15 FT1910 servos with a target budget under RMB 2,000 (about US$300).

My goal is to reproduce its core behaviors, including walking and rolling with reinforcement learning, then add navigation, new dance routines, and intelligent interaction.

Reinforcement learning · Legged robotics · Open-source hardware
